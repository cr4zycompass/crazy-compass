The journey

Your 6-day
mountain story.

A full Guwahati-to-Guwahati itinerary packed with valleys, waterfalls, monasteries, lakes and high-altitude Himalayan landscapes.

DAY 01

Guwahati → Sangti Valley

Check in at Sangti Valley and enjoy the mountain route.

Singhtein • Tenga Valley • Naag Mandir • Jameri Hanging Bridge • Nichiphula Waterfall • Kameng River
DAY 02

Sangti Valley → Tawang

Journey towards Tawang through some of Arunachal's most dramatic landscapes.

Mandala Top • Dirang Monastery • Jaswant Garh • War Memorial • Sela Pass • Sela Lake
DAY 03

Tawang Local Sightseeing

Explore the town, its spiritual landmarks and enjoy an evening Culture Show.

Tawang Monastery • Buddha Statue • War Memorial • Evening Culture Show
DAY 04

Bumla Pass Adventure

Head deeper into the high mountains and visit iconic border-region attractions.

Bumla Pass • Madhuri Lake • PTso Lake • Guru Duwara Temple
DAY 05

Tawang → Shergaon

Leave Tawang for a beautiful mountain stay in Shergaon.

Jang Waterfall • Sela Tunnel • Shergaon
DAY 06

Shergaon → Guwahati

Enjoy the final morning in the hills, then travel back to Guwahati for drop-off.

Morning departure • Guwahati drop
